## LinkedIn

Your property managers are spending 40% of their time doing things a software agent can handle in seconds.

Maintenance triage. Lease renewal outreach. Resident queries at 11pm. These are not judgment calls, they are repeatable workflows. And repeatable workflows are exactly what AI agents are built for.

Here is what the automation pipeline looks like in practice:

Maintenance: an agent receives the work order, classifies it by priority, assigns the right vendor, and confirms scheduling. No dispatcher in the loop. No delay between submission and assignment.

Lease renewals: the agent triggers outreach 90 days before expiry, tracks responses, and only flags a human when negotiation is actually needed. Everything else runs itself.

Resident communication: FAQs, package notifications, amenity booking, handled 24/7, at no incremental cost per query.

The result: property managers overseeing 500-1000 units consistently reclaim 12-15 hours per week. That is not a productivity tweak, that is a structural shift in how operations run.

Four integration layers make this possible: your property management software (Yardi, AppFolio, RealPage), maintenance vendor APIs, payment processors, and resident portals. The agents sit across all of them.

One rule applies everywhere: human escalation is non-negotiable. Eviction-adjacent situations, legal disputes, safety incidents. The agent flags them immediately. Everything else, it handles.
