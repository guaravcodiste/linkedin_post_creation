AI agents for wealth management address the admin-versus-judgment split directly through advisor augmentation: agents absorb the high-volume structured tasks, advisors focus on the conversations only they can have.

- Client communication prep: briefing time reduced from 90 minutes to 12 minutes.
- Robo-advisory: mass-affluent segment becomes economically viable with an automated onboarding, risk profiling, and rebalancing layer.

The architecture is not negotiable for compliance: client data stays in the firm's VPC. Every agent output has an advisor review checkpoint. Every decision is logged with the inputs and model version that produced it.
