## LinkedIn

Last-click attribution is not just inaccurate.

It actively misdirects budget by crediting the final touchpoint while ignoring the 6 to 12 touchpoints that built the intent that made the final click possible.

The paid search campaign you think is your best performer might be closing conversions that organic content, display, and email actually drove. Last-click gives all the credit to paid search, so you double down on paid and cut the channels that actually moved people down the funnel.

Here is what attribution that holds up in 2026 looks like:

-> Data-driven model using closed-loop conversion data as training signal
-> Shapley value approach for fair credit by causal contribution
-> Incrementality testing to validate what the model says
-> Budget reallocation from outcome data, not proxy signals

The model is not the hard part. The infrastructure is: you need a unified cross-channel event stream with timestamps, closed-loop conversion data within 48 hours, and at least 90 days of conversion history to train on.
