## LinkedIn

Most enterprise AI agent projects fail before they reach production.

And the failure happens at the vendor selection stage, not the build stage.

The vendor landscape in 2026 has over 400 firms claiming full-stack agentic capability. Fewer than 1 in 5 have actually shipped a production system at scale. The rest have shipped demos.

Here is the framework enterprise buyers need to tell the difference:

-> What to test: orchestration architecture, memory and state management, LLM routing strategy, and failure-mode engineering. A vendor missing any one of these is not a full-stack build partner.

-> Four shortlist filters: production deployment history (minimum 2 live agents), team composition with named engineers, vertical awareness, and a milestone-based engagement model.

-> The 30-day sandbox test: a capable vendor can deliver a scoped working agent on your real data structure within 30 days of engagement. This one test tells you more than any reference call or portfolio review.

-> Red flags to exit: fixed-scope contracts, case studies showing "AI features" instead of agent workflows, no named engineers for your project.

The demo proves nothing about what happens at step 47 of a 60-step workflow. Build your evaluation process before you build the agent.
