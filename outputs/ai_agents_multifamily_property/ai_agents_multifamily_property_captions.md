## LinkedIn

Your property managers don't have a staffing problem. They have a triage problem.

Maintenance requests, lease renewals, and resident messages can eat up to 40% of a regional property manager's week, before any actual strategic work starts. Most of that time isn't decision-making. It's manually moving information between a resident, a vendor, and a spreadsheet.

AI agents close that gap by running three workflows end to end:

→ Maintenance requests get classified by urgency and trade type, then dispatched to the right vendor automatically
→ Lease renewals get a defined outreach cadence at 120, 90, 60, and 45 days out, so no resident falls through the cracks
→ Resident messages get answered around the clock, with real status data pulled from your existing system

The difference between this and old-school automation is context. Rule-based systems see the word "water" and route every mention to emergency dispatch. An agent asks a clarifying question first, then routes correctly. That distinction is what makes triage actually reliable.

None of this requires ripping out Yardi, AppFolio, or Buildium. The agent connects through the API layer you already have.

Teams running this see administrative time drop by roughly a third to half, and resident response times move from hours to minutes.
