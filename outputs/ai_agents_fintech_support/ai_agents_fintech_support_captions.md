## LinkedIn

Your fintech support queue is growing faster than your headcount. Hiring your way out of it is not sustainable.

AI agents resolve the structured, deterministic queries that make up 70% of your Tier-1 volume: balance checks, transaction lookups, card freezes, dispute initiation, address updates. Each follows a defined resolution path. Zero judgment required.

Here is what the numbers look like in production at a 180,000-customer digital banking platform:

-> 47% of Tier-1 volume resolved autonomously, without any human involvement
-> Average resolution time: 72 seconds (down from 4.8 minutes)
-> CSAT: 4.4 out of 5 (up from 4.2, because customers prefer speed on structured queries)
-> Agent turnover: 28% annualized (down 14 points, because reps stopped reading transaction statuses all day)
-> Cost per resolution: down 72% on automated query types

The architecture that produces these results has three layers: intent classification (94% accuracy on the 11 most common query types), resolution execution via direct API calls, and sentiment-monitored handoff that triggers before the customer reaches frustration, not after.

The handoff design is where most implementations fail. Full conversation context must transfer to the human agent. If the customer has to repeat themselves, CSAT drops, not because AI was involved, but because the handoff was broken.
