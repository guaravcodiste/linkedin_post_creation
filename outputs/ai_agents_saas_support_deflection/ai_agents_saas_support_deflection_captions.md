## LinkedIn

Half your support queue is already answered. Your team just hasn't stopped answering it manually yet.

SaaS support teams are spending 40 to 60% of their ticket volume on questions that repeat every day: password resets, billing lookups, plan FAQs, status checks. These are not complex problems. They are structured, predictable, and entirely resolvable without a human in the loop.

That's where AI agents come in. But there's a version of this that works, and a version that tanks your CSAT.

The version that works is built on three distinct agent types:

- Knowledge-base agents that pull from your docs and close the loop with a real answer
- Workflow agents that actually execute actions via your product API, not just describe how to do them
- Routing agents that classify and assign tickets before a human ever reads them

The version that fails skips handoff design. When an agent can't resolve something and passes it to a human with no context, no summary, no history, your customer has to repeat everything. That gap is where satisfaction scores collapse.

Get the handoff right and the numbers flip. Teams using this approach see a 35% reduction in average handle time for tickets that do reach humans, because those tickets arrive pre-qualified and pre-contextualized.

The key rule: agents deflect structured, resolvable queries. Anything involving account security, fraud, emotional distress, or judgment goes to a human, every time.
