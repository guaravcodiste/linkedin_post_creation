## LinkedIn

Your CRM isn't broken. It's just out of date.

Enrichment, lead routing and forecast hygiene look like three different problems. They're not. All three read from the same decaying records, and none of them re-check the source.

B2B contact data decays roughly 20-25% a year. Nobody notices because the field still shows a value, it's just a value that stopped being true months ago. Then a lead lands on a Friday evening, gets routed on a stale territory field, and sits in the wrong queue until Monday while a competitor calls back in minutes.

Quarterly cleanups can't keep up with monthly decay. Asking reps to fix it collides with a week that's already mostly meetings and data entry, not selling.

What actually works is agents that run continuously instead of on a schedule, attach a confidence score and a source to every write, and flag conflicts instead of quietly resolving them.

Here's the order that matters:
→ Baseline your decay, response time and forecast variance
→ Fix enrichment first, it's the upstream dependency
→ Run in suggest mode before anything writes automatically
→ Promote to autonomy by confidence tier, not all at once

Routing and forecasting improve almost for free once the fields under them are current.

If a tool only fills more fields, it's a faster version of the problem. The ones worth building tell you which fields they don't trust, and why.
