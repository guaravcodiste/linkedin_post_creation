## LinkedIn

Your marketing ops team is not slow. Your handoffs are.

The median campaign launch takes 16 days from brief approval. Creative production accounts for 3 to 5 of those days. The other 11 are queue time between systems as someone manually moves data from one platform to the next.

AI agents eliminate that movement entirely.

Here is how a campaign ops agent works:

- Brief parsing node reads the approved brief and extracts objectives, audience parameters, channel requirements, and budget into structured fields automatically
- Audience segmentation node connects to your CDP, builds the segment, and validates size against benchmarks before anything moves forward
- Channel configuration node maps the campaign to each channel, configures UTM parameters, allocates budget, and prepares channel-specific creative specs
- Creative routing node sends requirements to the production team with due dates set based on campaign timeline

Human involvement is limited to two checkpoints: creative review and brand compliance before upload. Everything else runs without waiting for a person to copy and paste.
