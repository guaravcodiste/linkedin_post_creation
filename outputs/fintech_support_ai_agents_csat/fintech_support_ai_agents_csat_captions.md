## LinkedIn

Your AI support agent just deflected 500 tickets. Did you keep your customers, or quietly push them to a competitor?

The fintech companies getting this right are not deploying AI to handle everything. They are deploying it to handle the right things: balance inquiries, transaction status checks, password resets, card blocks. Structured, deterministic queries where speed beats empathy every time.

The ones getting it wrong are deflecting fraud complaints and account closure requests to bots. Those are the highest-churn moments in financial services. A customer who feels dismissed after a dispute is gone. No amount of fast response time repairs that.

The framework that works is simpler than most teams think:

- Deterministic queries: automate every time, no exceptions
- Ambiguous queries: apply routing logic, classify, hand off cleanly
- Emotional or complex queries: always escalate to a human with full context

That last part is where most implementations break. The handoff carries no context. The human asks the customer to repeat themselves. CSAT drops, not because AI was involved, but because the handoff was designed badly.

Done well, AI support in fintech reduces Tier-1 volume by 40 to 50 percent while improving CSAT on routine interactions. The stat is not aspirational. It is what happens when deflection matches query type.
