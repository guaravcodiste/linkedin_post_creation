## Topic: How to Vet an AI Agent Development Company in 2026

---

## LinkedIn

Most enterprise AI agent projects fail at vendor selection, not the build stage.

That is the uncomfortable truth. You spend months scoping requirements, write a detailed brief, and hand it to a vendor who has never shipped a production agentic system in their life. The demo they show you proves nothing about step 47 of a 60-step workflow.

Here is what the market actually looks like: fewer than 1 in 5 of the 400+ firms positioning themselves as AI agent developers have ever shipped an agentic system at scale. The rest are consultants with a Cursor subscription and a pitch deck.

Before you send a single RFP, run three filters:

- Production history: ask for two live agent deployments with workflow step counts and tool call volumes. No specifics means no capability.
- Team composition: confirm the team exists today. Orchestration engineer, prompt engineer, ML ops. A team that gets assembled after you sign is a team that has never worked together on your problem.
- Engagement model: fixed-scope contracts are incompatible with agentic builds. Demand milestone-based retainers tied to behavioral benchmarks, not deployment events.

Then run the 30-day sandbox test. One real workflow. 2 to 4 tool calls. Your actual data structure. Any hesitation is your answer.

Walk away from any firm that cannot name their orchestration framework, calls themselves an AI consulting company without a build portfolio, or ties delivery milestones to go-live dates rather than behavioral outcomes.

The right vendor does not just demo. They survive production.
